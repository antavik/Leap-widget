# Leap Widget User Manual

Leap Widget is a pixel-perfect calendar widget for the macOS Desktop and Notification
Center. It shows one or two months at a glance, matches the look of Classic
Calendar widgets, and opens the calendar app of your choice when you click it.

Leap Widget shows no events and asks for no permissions. It never connects to the
network, and it does not stay running in the background - macOS draws the
widgets for you.

## In a hurry

1. Open Leap Widget once. The window that appears walks you through the same three
   steps.
2. Control-click the Desktop, choose **Edit Widgets…**, search for *Leap Widget*, then
   drag out **Two Months** or **Month**.
3. Control-click the widget you placed and choose its **Edit** item to
   configure it.

You can quit Leap Widget afterward. The widgets keep working.

## Opening this manual again

Open Leap Widget and choose **Help ▸ Leap Widget Manual**, or press Command-? while Leap Widget is
in front. The manual is stored inside the app, so it works without a network
connection. The search field at the top of the Help menu searches these pages
by title and by their text.
