# Leap User Manual

Leap is a calendar widget for the macOS Desktop and Notification Center. It shows
one or two months at a glance, matches the look of Classic Calendar widget,
and opens the calendar app of your choice when you click it.

Leap shows no events and asks for no permissions. It never connects to the
network, and it does not stay running in the background — macOS draws the
widgets for you.

## In a hurry

1. Open Leap once. The window that appears is the three-step guide.
2. Right-click the Desktop, choose **Edit Widgets…**, search for *Leap*, and
   drag out **Two Months** or **Month**.
3. Right-click the widget you placed and choose its **Edit** item to configure it.

You can close Leap afterwards. The widgets keep working.

## Opening this manual again

Open Leap and choose **Help ▸ Leap Manual**, or press Command-? while Leap is
in front. The manual is inside the app, so it works without a network
connection. The search field at the top of the Help menu searches these pages
by name and by their text.
